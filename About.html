Bio & Values

People often describe me as a pragmatic, curious leader who isn't afraid to challenge the status quo. I love asking questions, exploring possibilities and encourage looking for better ways of solving problems.

✦

Beyond the screen

Born in India, raised in New Zealand, and having started my career in Australia, I now call London home. I think of myself as a global citizen, as every place I've lived has left its mark on who I am.

When I need to escape the screen, you'll usually find me painting, illustrating or trying my hand at pottery often inspired by my Indian roots. I also love to travel, hike and spend time by the ocean and true to my Kiwi and Aussie side - I take my coffee very seriously.

How I Lead

I aim to instill curiosity in the designers I lead, helping them ask better questions and adapt with confidence as the problems we're trying to solve keep changing with AI. The best solutions come from understanding people, systems and challenges deeply before jumping to answers.

Experience

Over the past 10+ years, I've worked across financial services and retail digital products in New Zealand, Australia and the UK.

Working in finance has taught me how to navigate ambiguity, regulation and competing priorities.

Outcome focused

Curiosity first
