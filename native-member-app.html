Case Study 01 — Product Strategy

Transforming a Web Experience into a Native Mobile Platform

Creating a native-first strategy for Smart Pension's member app to improve digital experiences, increase engagement and establish a scalable foundation for future products.

The opportunity

Smart Pension's member app was originally built as a responsive web experience inside a mobile wrapper. While this enabled speed initially, it created limitations as member expectations evolved.

"We weren't just rebuilding an app. We were redefining how members experience their pension."

The opportunity was to move beyond replicating web experiences and create a truly mobile-first financial experience built around native behaviours.

My role

As Design Manager, I created the design strategy for transitioning Smart Pension from a web-first approach to a native-first platform.

Strategy

  • Defined native-first vision
  • Created experience principles
  • Established migration approach

Leadership

  • Aligned Product and Engineering
  • Created shared direction
  • Supported design teams

Experience

  • Mobile interaction patterns
  • Journey redesign
  • Design quality

The challenge

Member experience

  • Web patterns on mobile
  • Limited native interactions
  • Inconsistent journeys

Business opportunity

  • Increase digital adoption
  • Reduce service dependency
  • Create new product opportunities

Platform limitations

  • Slow feature development
  • Limited mobile capability
  • No scalable native foundation

Reframing the problem

The initial question was:

"How do we improve the app?"

The question I introduced was:

"What should a modern pension experience feel like on mobile?"

This shifted the conversation from incremental improvements to a long-term product strategy.

Design principles

Design for mobile behaviour

Move away from desktop patterns and embrace native interactions.

Build scalable foundations

Create reusable native components that support future growth.

Simplify complexity

Pensions are complicated. The experience shouldn't be.

Prioritise member value

Focus investment on journeys that create the biggest impact.

The transformation

Key member journeys were redesigned and migrated into native experiences, moving beyond simply replicating web screens.

Native journeys

Mobile-first experiences built around platform capabilities.

Design system

Reusable components and patterns for scalable delivery.

Future platform

Foundation for new products and personalised experiences.

Impact

Improved trust

App rating increased from 3.6 to 4.7 stars.

Better experience

Members gained a more modern, mobile-first way to manage pensions.

Strategic foundation

Created the foundation for future digital products and experiences.

"Design leadership is about recognising when the current experience no longer serves customers — and creating the vision for what comes next."
