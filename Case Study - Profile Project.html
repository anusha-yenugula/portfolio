Case Study 02

Building a Person-Centric Platform

Transforming Smart Pension's identity model to create a single customer profile, reduce operational costs, and establish the foundation for AI-powered customer experiences.

Overview

As Smart Pension expanded, customers increasingly held multiple pension accounts due to job changes and new products. The platform treated every account as a separate customer, creating fragmented data, duplicated communications, and increasing operational costs.

Rather than redesigning onboarding, I led the strategic initiative to redefine how identity worked across the platform — shifting Keystone from an account-centric architecture to a person-centric model.

Strategy

Core principles

  • One verified profile per customer
  • Multiple accounts linked together
  • Email becomes the primary identifier
  • Personal data managed once
  • Progressive profile creation

Key capabilities

  • Automated account matching
  • Employer management tools
  • Admin tooling
  • Email verification
  • Account claiming
  • Unified customer identity

Business impact

Operational efficiency

Reduced support effort, fewer manual interventions and lower administration costs.

Better customer experience

One login, one profile and a seamless experience across multiple pension accounts and future products.

Platform foundation

Established trusted customer identity to enable Project Optima, AI-powered service and future financial products.

"This project wasn't about improving onboarding. It was about changing how the organisation thought about customer identity — creating a strategic platform capability that will enable every future customer experience."
