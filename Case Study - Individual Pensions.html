Reimagining Keystone: From Workplace Pension Administration to a Financial Platform

The opportunity

Keystone was originally built around workplace and employer-based master trust arrangements. This model allowed Smart Pension to successfully serve employers and employees, but it limited the platform's ability to support customers outside an employment relationship.

"The challenge was not launching another pension product. It was redesigning the platform so it could support a new relationship between Smart Pension and customers."

The opportunity was to evolve Keystone into a flexible savings platform capable of supporting individual customers, direct funding and future financial products.

Design principles

Design for flexibility

Create foundations that support multiple products, markets and customer models.

Enable direct relationships

Build for tomorrow
